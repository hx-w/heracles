# Exercise media and attribution

Heracles includes a small curated selection of wger exercise records, rather than a complete gym database. Each entry retains its source page, source identifier, author and per-file licence in `internal/app/assets/exercises.json`. The same attribution is available in the interface under 素材与许可.

- Chinese instructions are adaptations and additions, distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The exercise data is separately licensed from the MIT application code.
- Original images retain their individual CC BY-SA 3.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0 licences and credits. Files flagged as AI generated by the source were excluded.
- Selected videos were transcoded to H.264 MP4, scaled for phone playback and stripped of audio. One short GIF and one video poster were derived from credited video sources. These adaptations retain the source video licence.
- The catalogue contains 12 exercises, 12 images, 5 videos and 1 GIF. Media is bundled locally, so opening a private workout does not contact an external image host.
- Teaching material is a general demonstration. Equipment models and individual movement limits differ. It is not a diagnosis or a guarantee that the viewer is using the machine correctly.

Source: [wger public exercise API](https://wger.de/api/v2/exerciseinfo/) and [project licence documentation](https://wger.readthedocs.io/en/latest/). Source URLs and authors are included per asset; do not remove them when replacing a file. No ExerciseDB/GymVisual content is included.
